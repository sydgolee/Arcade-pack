PRIVACY

Last updated 27 November 2026

Short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, no cookies. Your saves stay on your device. If you put your name on a leaderboard, that name, your score and your country go on the public online boards. You can switch that off at any time.

WHO WE ARE

The 8-Bit Arcade Pack is made by Syd Golee in Denmark, who is responsible for the data described here. Questions: s.golee@icloud.com.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

To remember your progress, the games save these in your browser's local storage on this device only:

  • saved runs, records, game history and best scores
  • this device's leaderboards, trophies, play time and settings
  • a random player ID (a long code with no link to who you are)
  • an offline copy of the game files, so the pack works without a connection

ONLINE LEADERBOARDS

When you enter a leaderboard name and Online leaderboards is on in Settings, each score you set is sent to our leaderboard server. We store:

  • the name you chose (up to 12 letters, shown publicly)
  • the game, your score and a short result such as "room 14"
  • your country, worked out from your connection at that moment, so we can show country boards (we do not store your IP address)
  • your random player ID and the time of the score

The legal basis is your choice to take part (consent). Do not use your full real name if you would rather stay anonymous. The server runs on Cloudflare (Workers and D1), which processes the requests for us under the Cloudflare Privacy Policy. Scores are kept until you delete them or the leaderboards close. We never sell the data and never show ads.

PLAYING A FRIEND ONLINE

When you start or join an online game, your leaderboard name, your random player ID and your moves are sent through our game server (Cloudflare) to your friend. The server keeps the moves of that game so you can reconnect, and deletes the whole game automatically 24 hours after it was last joined. Anyone with your invite code can join while a seat is free, so only share it with people you want to play.

DELETING YOUR DATA

In Settings: switch off Online leaderboards to stop sending scores, tap Delete my online scores to remove everything this device sent, and tap Reset all progress to wipe this device. You can also email us and we will delete it for you.

HOSTING

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub (or itch.io, if you play there) may log basic technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads the files, for security and to keep the service running. That is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs.

CHILDREN

Players under 13 should only use online leaderboards with a parent's permission, or keep them switched off in Settings. Some games include cartoon pixel shooting.

YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR you can ask to see, correct or delete your online leaderboard entries, and withdraw consent at any time. Contact us at the address above. You can also complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too.
