PRIVACY

Last updated 27 November 2026

Short version: no account, no ads, no tracking, no cookies. Your saves and scores stay on your own device.

WHO WE ARE

The 8-Bit Arcade Pack is made by Syd Golee in Denmark. Questions: s.golee@icloud.com.

WHAT WE COLLECT

Nothing. The games do not send any information about you or your play to us or to anyone else. There are no analytics, ads, social plugins or third-party scripts. The fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

WHAT STAYS ON YOUR DEVICE

To remember your progress, the games save a few things in your browser's local storage on this device only:

  • saved runs, records and best scores for each game
  • the local leaderboards, including the name you type in
  • awards, play time and your settings
  • an offline copy of the game files, so the pack works without a connection

We cannot see any of it. It never leaves your device unless you share a screenshot or a score yourself.

DELETING YOUR DATA

Go to Settings and tap Reset all progress, or clear this website's data in your browser settings. Uninstalling the home-screen app also removes it.

HOSTING

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like every web host, GitHub may log basic technical data such as your IP address when your browser loads the files, for security and to keep the service running. That is covered by the GitHub Privacy Statement. We do not receive those logs.

CHILDREN

The games collect no personal data, so they are fine for players of any age. Some games include cartoon pixel shooting.

YOUR RIGHTS

Under the GDPR you can ask what data we hold about you. The answer is none, because everything stays on your device. You can still contact us at the address above, and you can complain to the Danish Data Protection Agency (Datatilsynet).

CHANGES

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes too. If we ever add something like online leaderboards, it will be optional and explained here first.
