ABOUT

v · free · no ads · works offline

The 8-Bit Arcade Pack is six small games in one cartridge shelf, made to feel like the consoles of the 1980s: 16 colours, chunky pixels, beeps and square waves. Every game runs in your browser on phone or computer. There is nothing to sign up for.

THE GAMES

Dungeon of One Room: one room, endless floors. Every room you clear adds a rule that never goes away.
Wall Maze: a race-and-block strategy duel on a 9×9 board.
Fleet Strike: hide your fleet, hunt theirs, beat three admirals.
High Noon Rider: an endless western run-and-gun, from noon to starry night.
Tommy Gun Alley: an endless 1929 Chicago shootout, from midnight to grey dawn.
8-Ball: pool hall classic against three CPU players or a friend.

PLAY IT LIKE AN APP

iPhone: in Safari tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
Android: in Chrome tap ⋮, then Install app or Add to Home screen.
It then opens full screen and works with no connection.
